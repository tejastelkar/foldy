How it works

IOKit Lid Sensor
Reads the hardware hinge angle directly from the Mac's embedded IOKit HID sensor. Zero polling, zero CPU when idle, zero battery drain.
Metal 3 Pipeline
Hardware-accelerated ScreenCaptureKit frame projection. Live desktop windows and wallpaper tilt, fold, and shade in unified memory at 120Hz ProMotion.
Three Fluid Styles
Silk, Shade, and Frost. Customize perspective pitch, 12-tap Poisson disc bokeh, liquid glass chromatic dispersion, and ambient horizon shadows.
Mechanical Audio
A crisp, soft haptic sound when the lid opens and the desktop clears. Switch it off in General settings anytime.
Quiet Menu Bar
Lives unobtrusively in your macOS menu bar. Pause with a click or configure whenever you need.

Requirements

Operating System
macOS 14 Sonoma, macOS 15 Sequoia, or newer.
Hardware
Apple Silicon MacBook (MacBook Air or MacBook Pro with M1, M2, M3, or M4 chip) equipped with a lid angle sensor.
Permissions
Screen Recording access (strictly used in GPU RAM for the tilt shader; frames never leave memory).

Private by Design

100% On-Device
Screen frames are captured directly to Metal textures in unified memory and discarded instantly. Nothing is ever saved to disk, tracked, or sent over the network.
Zero Telemetry
No user tracking, no analytics SDKs, no accounts required. Free, open source, and completely transparent.
